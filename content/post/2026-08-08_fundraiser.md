I'm participating in a fundraiser walk ([at the Milwaukee
Zoo on 2026-08-29](https://give.primaryimmune.org/event/milwaukee-walk-and-community-days/e754827)) for the [Immune Deficiency
Foundation](https://primaryimmune.org), which has been a great source
of information and support as I've dealt with
[CVID](https://kbroman.org/blog/2025/11/28/cvid/) and now also
[GLILD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granulomatous%E2%80%93lymphocytic_interstitial_lung_disease).

I've particularly appreciated their [Patient & Family
Handbook](https://primaryimmune.org/index%2Ephp/resources/print-material/patient-family-handbook-primary-immunodeficiency-seventh-edition-preview),
their many [webinars on
youtube](https://www.youtube.com/@immunedeficiencyfoundation), and
their [online support groups](https://idffriends.org) (particularly
valuable for a rare disease).

Please visit my [fundraising
page](https://give.primaryimmune.org/kbroman) and consider supporting
the Immune Deficiency Foundation.
