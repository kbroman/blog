Nearly 20% of my publications are in the journal
_[Genetics](https://academic.oup.com/genetics)_. It's long been my
favorite journal. It's a peer-edited,
[society](https://genetics-gsa.org) journal covering the full range of
genetics, including statistical and computational methods. Authors are
encouraged to include the full details (for example, of methods).

When I started reading _Genetics_, it was only available in print.
It's now solely online, and they scanned all and posted all of the
articles going back to the [first
issue](https://academic.oup.com/genetics/issue/1/1) from
January, 1916. (The [first
paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/genetics/1.1.1) was "Non-disjunction as
proof of the chromosome theory of heredity," by Calvin Bridges.)

All of those old papers, in fact all papers at _Genetics_ more than a
year old, have long been openly available on their website. So you
didn't need access to a subscription to read and download [Haldane and
Waddington's 1931 paper on Inbreeding and
linkage](https://doi.org/10.1093/genetics/16.5.504a) (a particular
favorite of mine), or [Lander and Botstein's 1989 paper on interval
mapping](https://doi.org/10.1093/genetics/121.1.185).

But, alas, the policy changed at the beginning of 2025, and papers
older than 15 years are available only with a subscription. Just the
papers that are 1-15 years old are openly available. So 18 of the 32 papers
I've published in _Genetics_ are no available to most people.

It has something to do with a change at [Counter
Metrics](https://www.countermetrics.org/), which is used to track
usage of journal subscriptions.

I'm sad.

Since _Genetics_ moved to [Oxford University Press](oup.com), the cost
of publishing in the journal has become outlandish. For GSA members,
it [costs
$3895](https://academic.oup.com/genetics/pages/general-instructions#Manuscript-Charges)
to publish open access.
Even publishing in _[G3](https://academic.oup.com/g3journal)_ now
[costs
$2330](https://academic.oup.com/g3journal/pages/general-instructions#Manuscript-Charges)
for GSA members.

This sucks. Let's make it all [Diamond
Open Access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_open_access).
